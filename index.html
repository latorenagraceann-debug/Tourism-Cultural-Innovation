A WEEK THAT BRINGS US TOGETHER

Meet the
Moriones.

Each Holy Week, the island’s streets become a stage for the Moriones tradition. Masked figures called moryon bring the story of Longinus to life through processions, pageantry, and devotion.

Dates and activities vary each year. Check with local tourism offices before planning your visit.
